# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We welcome participation in a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, assume good faith while addressing impact, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. There is no public maintainer contact configured yet; this policy must be reviewed and a private reporting route published before the first public release.

This short initial policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant. The canonical text and enforcement contacts should be reviewed before public adoption.
