## Remaining gates

- Independent protocol/threat review of invite replay/theft, expiry, consent, journal recovery, cleanup, and the strength of the restart-recovery claims.
- Final maintainer signoff after that review.
- Separate M1 acceptance: transfer between two physical devices over a LAN with Internet disabled, with matching digests, cancellation, and bounded-resource evidence.

The product guarantee remains recovery after connection/process interruption. It does not include power-loss durability.
